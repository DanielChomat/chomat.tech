Privacy Policy

Patrin does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

No networking code

The app contains no networking code: no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts, no servers. It cannot send data anywhere, because the capability does not exist in the app. Currency conversion uses exchange-rate tables bundled inside the app itself.

Your data, your device

All assets, liabilities, and value history you enter are stored only on your device, protected by iOS encryption and the app's biometric lock. The developer has no access to your data and no technical means of obtaining it.

Backups

The only way data leaves your device is the encrypted backup file you create yourself. Backups are encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. Nobody — including the developer — can read a backup or recover a lost passphrase.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives only Apple's confirmation that the purchase was made; no payment or identity information reaches the app or the developer.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be stated plainly in the app's release notes.
