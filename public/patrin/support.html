Support

Patrin keeps all your data on your device, so there is no account to recover and no server-side help needed — but if something isn't working, or you have a question or an idea, get in touch.

Email patrinapp@protonmail.com

I forgot my backup passphrase

Backup passphrases cannot be recovered — by design. Nobody, including the developer, can decrypt a backup without its passphrase. If your data is still on your device, create a new backup with a new passphrase and store it in your password manager.

I got a new phone

Patrin's data does not travel through iCloud or device transfers — that is the privacy promise working as intended. Create an encrypted backup on your old device (Settings → Export), move the file to the new device, and import it there.

Restoring a purchase

If you've already bought the one-time unlock, open Settings → Restore Purchase on any device signed into the same Apple Account.

What happened to my values after an update?

Nothing leaves or changes without you: the app only ever shows values you entered. If a number looks wrong, check the asset's event timeline on its detail screen — every recorded value is listed there and can be edited.
